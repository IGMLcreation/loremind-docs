First week after the 1.0 launch, and it was a busy one: thank you to everyone who installed
DM Loremind, tested it, and above all **reported bugs** — almost everything below comes straight
from your feedback.

{/* truncate */}

## 🎲 Foundry VTT import: the big batch (module 0.2.8 → 0.2.9)

This was the main pain point several of you reported, and it got a deep treatment:

- **Dungeon Alchemist walls**: imported scenes triggered validation errors in Foundry
  (`light: 2 is not a valid choice`) — the walls used the old Foundry v9 scale. They are now
  converted properly, and as a bonus "terrain" walls (wagons, rocks) partially let vision
  through, as Dungeon Alchemist intended.
- **DungeonDraft lights**: no more completely blinding screen — colors were decoded incorrectly
  (torches turned pure white) and the intensity stacked on top of the lighting already baked into
  the image. Lights got their true colors back, with a subtle intensity.
- **Scene previews**: missing thumbnails in the Foundry sidebar are now generated systematically.
- No more "disallowed extension" error on `.dd2vtt` files during import.
- **Enemies without a Foundry actor**: if an enemy references an actor that doesn't exist in your
  world (created in another world, missing compendium), the module now creates a **blank sheet to
  fill in** instead of silently skipping the enemy — and the stats you enter survive re-imports.
  A notification lists the enemies concerned.

## 🧹 "Ghost quests" are gone

A sneaky bug: deleting a free quest could leave its scenes lying around in an invisible corner of
the database… which is why deleted quests kept resurrecting in the Foundry export. Three changes:

- Deleting a free quest now **really** deletes its content — and the confirmation dialog tells you
  the exact impact ("4 scenes will be deleted with it") before you confirm.
- The Foundry export only includes quests that actually exist.
- On first launch of 1.0.3, an **automatic repair** takes care of existing data: orphaned content
  becomes visible again as a quest (nothing is destroyed behind your back — you decide whether to
  keep it), and empty shells are cleaned up.

## 🐛 Other fixes

- **Backup/restore**: images were not properly linked to the campaign when importing a backup,
  which broke their display — fixed (1.0.1).
- **Lore sidebar**: with many templates, the bottom panel would end up hiding the whole tree.
  It is now capped with its own scrollbar — thanks to the user who reported it 🙏.

## 🔒 Under the hood: security and quality

- A continuous analysis pipeline is now in place (full code linting + vulnerability scanning of the
  Docker images): **22 CVEs purged** from the images on the very first run, and every build is now
  checked before publishing.
- A big technical-debt pass on the application core, to keep the code healthy over time.

## How to update

- **Docker**: `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d` (or the automatic update);
- **Foundry module**: update from Foundry's module administration (v0.2.9).

And if you run into a bug, come tell the tale on [the Discord](https://discord.gg/cPpFzCjEzQ) —
as you can see above, it works. See you next week!
