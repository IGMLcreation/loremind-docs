# Edit, illustrate and link pages

After [creating a page](/docs/lore/creation-page), open it and click **Edit**.
Its [template](/docs/lore/templates) defines the available fields and their layout.

## Fill the sheet

- **Text**: write the block's content.
- **Key/value list**: enter values beside the template's fixed labels. Edit the
  template to add a label.
- **Table**: add rows and fill the template's columns. Deleting a row removes its values.
- **Image**: add illustrations and adjust their framing.

Click **Save** before leaving the editor. **Cancel** returns to the sheet without
saving form changes.

## Add illustrations

Image upload areas accept file selection and drag-and-drop. Supported formats are
**JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF**, up to **10 MB per image**. Sheet illustrations and
battlemaps serve different purposes: use the dedicated campaign scene section for
combat maps.

Inside a Lore Image block, use the carousel to browse images, drag an image to
adjust its crop and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Framing is stored per image and page.

## Tags and related pages

**Tags** describe a page with keywords. **Related pages** reference other pages
in the setting: a city can link to its ruling faction, and a character to their birthplace.

Select pages in the picker, then save. Links use page IDs, so renaming a target
preserves the relation. Removing a link does not delete its target page.

Page-to-page links appear in the [relationship graph](/docs/campagne/graphe-liens)
of a campaign associated with that setting. To link a scene or NPC to the setting,
use **Related Lore pages** on that entity's sheet.

## Move a page

Change its **folder** in the editor or move it in the [tree](/docs/navigation).
The page's template and content are independent of its folder.

## Private notes and sharing

**Private notes** separate secrets from descriptive content. This helps organise
reading and exports; it does not create a player area protected by separate access
permissions. Read [Data and sharing](/docs/configuration/donnees-confidentialite)
before distributing exports.

For AI enrichment, see [Lore page generation](/docs/assistant-ia/generation-pages).
