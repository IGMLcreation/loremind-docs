# Prepare a module to share

:::info[Beta]
The publishing sheet and PDF options were added on **beta**, after the
**1.3.0-beta** milestone. If these controls are missing, see
[beta additions](/docs/configuration/nouveautes-beta).
:::

The **publishing sheet** adds a cover, an introduction for another GM and
credits to your campaign. Use it to produce a PDF module to share or sell.
Every field is optional: an empty sheet keeps the usual campaign booklet
presentation.

## Open and save the sheet

From **Export to PDF** on the campaign home, choose **Fill in the sheet**, or
**Edit the sheet** if it already contains information.

Expand the sections you need, fill them in, then click **Save**.
**Export to PDF** on this sheet opens the booklet options. Generating the PDF
first saves your changes. If saving fails, export stops rather than using an
older sheet.

## Cover and technical sheet

| Section | Fields | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Cover** | Subtitle, author, version, illustration | Present the module under the campaign title; a portrait image is recommended |
| **Technical page** | Game system, character levels, number of players, estimated length | Help a GM decide whether the adventure fits their table |

These are free-text fields: for example, enter “1 to 3” for levels and “Two
4-hour sessions” for duration. The **Game system** field describes the module;
it does not replace the campaign's link to a [game system](/docs/game-system).

## Presentation and GM introduction

- **The adventure at a glance**: the situation and play experience on offer.
  When empty, the PDF uses the campaign description.
- **Content warnings**: sensitive themes covered in the adventure.
- **Hidden background**: what has already happened, the opponents and their plans.
- **Hooks**: concrete reasons for the characters to get involved.

Lightbulbs beside writing fields offer guidance. Lines starting with `-`
become bullet points in the PDF.

Hidden background and hooks are reserved for the **GM edition**. The presentation
and warnings also appear in the **player edition**: write them with the intended
audience in mind.

## Credits and licence

Use **Credits** for writing, illustrations, maps, editing and playtesting.
**Licence and legal notice** holds reuse terms and notices for content you use.
These texts appear on the PDF credits page. Filling in the sheet does not
publish anything online.

## Choose what to share

From the sheet or campaign home, **Export to PDF** lets you select the GM or
player edition, included parts, maps and paper format.
See [Campaign PDF booklet](/docs/export/pdf) for filters and appendices.

To transfer a campaign that can be **edited in Loremind**, use the
[ZIP content export](/docs/export). The publishing sheet and its illustration
are included in exported campaign data; include images to transfer the cover.
A PDF is a reading document, not an importable backup.
