# Choose a guided method

:::info[Beta 1.4.0]
The three-method chooser is available from **1.4.0-beta**. Earlier beta builds
may open Five-room dungeon directly. These workflows run **without AI**:
you write the answers.
:::

On the campaign home, choose **Guided method**, or **Write an adventure step by
step** when the campaign is empty. **Write with a guided method** presents three
cards. Click **Start** under the one you want.

## Choose a structure

| Method | Use | Workflow | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Five-room dungeon](/docs/campagne/methode-cinq-salles) | A short adventure in five beats | 9 steps | 1 chapter and 5 scenes, with optional sequential links |
| [Node-based investigation](/docs/campagne/methode-enquete) | Several leads to discover a truth | 9 steps | 1 chapter, 6 scenes and 12 lead/clue links |
| [The villain's plan](/docs/campagne/methode-plan-mechant) | An enemy whose plan progresses during the campaign | 10 steps | 1 chapter and 6 scenes; optional six-segment clock |

An **NPC** scene type describes a meeting; add a character sheet afterwards under
[Characters](/docs/campagne/personnages). You can also complete illustrations,
linked opponents and read-aloud text after creation.

## Place the adventure

The first step offers an **existing linear arc** or the name of a **new arc**.
Hub arcs are not offered as destinations. The new chapter is appended after
existing chapters; a new arc follows the campaign's other arcs.

Fields marked with an asterisk are required to continue. Suggested scene names
remain editable. **Previous** and **Next** let you revisit answers; lightbulbs
open the mentor's guidance.

## Review and create

The final step summarises the chapter, scenes and types, plus planned links
or a clock. **Create the adventure** saves the result and opens the chapter
for further editing. Existing sheets are preserved.

Sequential linking is an option for **Five-room dungeon**. The investigation
builds its own network; the villain's plan leaves scenes unlinked so you can
trigger them across sessions.

## Resume a draft

The current step and answers are stored **in this browser**, for this campaign
and method, when local storage is available. Leave the workflow and reopen the
same card to resume it. Each method has its own draft; PC and tablet drafts
do not synchronise.

**Start over** clears answers after confirmation. A failed creation preserves
them for retry; successful creation removes the draft. Until final confirmation,
no new chapter or scene is created.

After writing the chapter, use [Editor review](/docs/campagne/relecture-editeur)
to examine choices, stakes and access paths.
