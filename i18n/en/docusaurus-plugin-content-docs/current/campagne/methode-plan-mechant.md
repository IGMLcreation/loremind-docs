# Guided method: The villain's plan

:::info[Beta 1.4.0]
Available from **1.4.0-beta**, without an AI provider.
:::

Prepare what the enemy will do unless the characters stop them: six stages,
three discreet followed by three visible. Each leaves a trace and gives players
an opportunity to intervene.

On the campaign home, open **Guided method**, choose **The villain's plan**,
then **Start**. Shared placement and draft behaviour is covered in
[Choose a guided method](/docs/campagne/methodes-guidees).

## The ten workflow steps

| Step | Preparation |
|---|---|
| **Placement** | Existing linear arc or new arc |
| **The enemy** | Plan title, enemy, goal, stakes and possible player objectives |
| **1 — Prepare in secret** | First discreet action |
| **2 — Gain allies** | Support gained and people affected |
| **3 — Set the trap** | Final preparations before visible action |
| **4 — Strike** | The plan becomes public |
| **5 — Crush the resistance** | The enemy targets those who can still stop them |
| **6 — Reach the goal** | What happens if nobody prevents it |
| **The plan's clock** | Optional clock creation and its name |
| **Summary** | Chapter, six typed scenes and optional clock |

For each stage of the plan, choose **Event**, **NPC**, **Location** or
**Encounter**, then describe the action and what players can notice and do.
Initially suggested types vary across stages.

## What gets saved

**Create the adventure** adds **one chapter and six unlinked scenes**. The chapter
uses the enemy and goal as its description, along with player objectives, stakes
and a numbered plan in its GM notes.

Each scene receives the enemy action as its description and possible signs or
responses under **Choices and consequences**. The enemy name is used to write
the plan; create their sheet under [Characters](/docs/campagne/personnages).

## The six-segment clock

The default option to create a six-segment clock adds one with your chosen name
to **each existing playthrough** of the campaign. Progress stays independent in
each playthrough. Stakes become the clock description.

If no playthrough exists, no clock is created. A playthrough added later does
not receive one automatically: create it under
[playthrough clocks](/docs/campagne/horloges). Choose the option to track the plan
yourself to create only the chapter and scenes.

The clock is **manual**, without an automatic trigger. Advance it when the enemy
makes progress and play the relevant scene when appropriate. Segments do not
start scenes, and ending a session does not advance the plan on its own.

## Adapt the plan during play

If characters thwart a stage, revise the next actions or slow the clock. These
six scenes describe the enemy's initial intentions rather than events imposed
on the players. Record the consequences in the playthrough journal.
