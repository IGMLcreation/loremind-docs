# Guided method: Five-room dungeon

:::info[Beta]
This method belongs to the writing mentor on **beta**, after stable 1.0.3.
You write the answers yourself; no AI provider is required.
:::

The method guides a short adventure through five highlights. The “dungeon” can
be a crypt, ship, forest or masked ball.

## Start

On the campaign home, click **Guided method**, or **Write an adventure step by
step** on an empty campaign. The **Five-room dungeon** wizard opens.

![First step and destination arc, French UI](/img/screenshots/beta/methode-cinq-salles.jpg)

Each screen explains its purpose and offers an example. Some lightbulbs open
mentor tips. Use **Previous** and **Next** to revise your answers.

## The nine steps

| Step | What you prepare |
|---|---|
| **Placement** | An existing linear arc or the name of a new one |
| **Frame** | Adventure name, location and hook |
| **Objectives and stakes** | What players seek and what happens if they fail |
| **Room 1: entrance and guardian** | Initial obstacle, approaches, possible enemies and secrets |
| **Room 2: challenge** | Puzzle, negotiation or exploration and its outcomes |
| **Room 3: setback** | Trap, complication or unwelcome surprise |
| **Room 4: climax** | Confrontation, opponent's goal and alternatives to fighting |
| **Room 5: reward or revelation** | Gains, consequences and an opening for the next adventure |
| **Review** | Summary and the option to connect scenes |

Room names have editable defaults. Optional details can be completed later.
A **Hub** arc cannot receive this method: choose a linear arc or create one.

## What is created

**Create adventure** adds **one chapter and five scenes** to the chosen arc,
or first creates the requested new linear arc. The chapter receives the name,
hook, objectives and stakes. Scenes receive descriptions, choices, GM notes,
optional enemies and the shared location.

The first two scene types follow the chosen nature; the others are an **Event**,
**Encounter** and **Revelation**. With **Link rooms in order** enabled by default,
four “To the next room” branches connect the five scenes. Revise these later in
the [scene map](/docs/campagne/branches).

:::note[Five scenes rather than five room records]
The method's “rooms” are **chapter scenes**. It does not create five rooms inside
one scene or draw a battlemap. For that workflow, see
[Explorable locations and dungeon rooms](/docs/campagne/donjons).
:::

Content is added to the existing campaign. The chapter opens after creation so
you can refine sheets, link bestiary entries and add branches. Then consult
[Editor review](/docs/campagne/relecture-editeur).

## Resume a draft

Answers and the current step are stored **in this browser**, per campaign and
method, when local storage is available. This is not a campaign backup and does
not sync between PC and tablet.

**Restart** clears the draft after confirmation. Failed creation keeps answers
for retrying; successful creation clears the draft. No new campaign sheets are
created before the final button.
