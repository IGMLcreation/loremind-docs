# Writing mentor

:::info[Availability]
This guide covers the mentor present in development sources. If your installation
does not show lightbulb buttons, your version does not include it yet.
:::

The mentor provides built-in advice while writing arcs, chapters and scenes.
It works **without AI or network requests**.

## Open a tip

In an arc, chapter or scene editor, click the **lightbulb** beside a field label
or section title. The panel contains:

- the field's purpose in preparation;
- questions to get started;
- a concrete example;
- a common pitfall.

Click another lightbulb to change the topic, or the close button to dismiss the
panel. Clicking the current topic's lightbulb again also closes it.

## Covered topics

| Sheet | Advice |
|---|---|
| **Arc** | Synopsis, themes, stakes, rewards and resolution |
| **Chapter** | Synopsis, player objectives and narrative stakes |
| **Scene** | Description, node type, place and time, atmosphere, narration, secrets, choices, branches and combat |

Optional sections remain collapsible. An indicator on their header shows when
they already contain content.

## Use it during preparation

For “Choices and consequences”, start with two possible approaches, their costs
and what happens on failure. Write your answers in the field. The mentor does
not fill the form or score your writing.

[Guided preparation](/docs/campagne/preparation) detects structural gaps; the mentor
helps you write; the [AI assistant](/docs/assistant-ia) suggests content on request.
Each aid can be used independently.
