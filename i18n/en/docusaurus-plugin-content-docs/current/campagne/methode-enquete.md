# Guided method: Node-based investigation

:::info[Beta 1.4.0]
Available from **1.4.0-beta**, without AI or an API key.
:::

Prepare several ways to discover the same truth: a hook, three interconnected
leads, a conclusion and a fallback scene. Players can switch leads when one
approach fails.

From **Guided method** on the campaign home, choose **Node-based investigation**,
then **Start**. For placement and drafts, see
[Choose a guided method](/docs/campagne/methodes-guidees).

## The nine steps

| Step | What you prepare |
|---|---|
| **Placement** | Existing linear arc or new arc |
| **The mystery** | Investigation title, hidden truth and stakes |
| **The hook** | An event or meeting that opens the case, with initial leads |
| **First lead** | A location, person or event; content, clues and secrets |
| **Second lead** | Another source, its clues and secrets |
| **Third lead** | A third entry point for varied approaches |
| **The conclusion** | Revelation or confrontation, with possible consequences |
| **The fallback** | An event the GM can trigger when play stalls, offering new leads |
| **Summary** | Chapter, six typed scenes and links to create |

The three leads offer **Location**, **NPC** or **Event** types. Initial choices
vary to encourage different sources. Suggested names can be edited like any
other answer.

## The resulting network

**Create the adventure** adds a chapter and **six scenes**, with **twelve links**:

- the hook opens all three leads;
- the leads form a loop: 1 → 2 → 3 → 1;
- each lead reaches the conclusion through a **Clue** link;
- the fallback points to all three leads.

Other connections are **Lead** links. The conclusion therefore has three distinct
source scenes. In the [scene map](/docs/campagne/branches), replace generic labels
with concrete clues and adjust paths to fit your plot.

The mystery's truth goes into the **chapter's GM notes**. Secrets from the three
leads become **secret GM notes** on their scenes; written clues are kept under
**Choices and consequences**. This method does not automatically fill the arc's
Hidden truth field.

## Run the investigation

The fallback is an **Event** scene to trigger when useful: a witness comes forward,
the enemy retaliates or a threat advances. Time or a failed roll does not
automatically trigger it.

The network supplies paths; write what characters can actually learn from each
lead. [Editor review](/docs/campagne/relecture-editeur) flags revelations with too
few access paths and clues coming entirely from the same kind of scene.
