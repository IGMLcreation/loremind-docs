# Clocks and threats

Clocks track growing pressure: reinforcements, a watch investigation, a ritual or
ship repairs. They belong to a **playthrough**, so each group's progress is independent.

## Create a clock

Open a playthrough from **Campaign → My playthroughs**, then **Progress clocks**.
The same controls are available in the **Playthrough** tab of
[session mode](/docs/campagne/mode-seance).

![Playthrough preparation and clock form, French UI](/img/screenshots/guides/partie-horloges.jpg)

Enter a **name**, choose **2, 4, 6, 8, 10 or 12 segments**, select a trigger and
click **Add**. Use the pencil to edit a clock and **+ / −** to adjust its progress
within its segment limits.

## Choose a trigger

| Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Manual** | Only your adjustments change progress |
| **When a fact becomes true** | Adds one segment when the selected fact is activated |
| **When a quest is completed** | Adds one segment when the selected quest becomes completed |
| **At session end** | Adds one segment when a session in this playthrough ends |

For a fact, enter its name; for a quest, select it from the list.
[Facts](/docs/campagne/parties) appear automatically when a condition or trigger
references them.

Automatic clocks remain manually adjustable. Triggers react to **events**, not
elapsed real time: “at session end” does not mean “every evening”.

## Group clocks into a threat

Enter a threat name and click **Add threat**. Select it when creating or editing
a clock. For example, “The cult organises” might group “Recruitment” and “Ritual
preparation”.

A threat groups clocks; each clock keeps its own trigger and counter. Deleting
a threat preserves its clocks, moving them into **Free clocks**.

## Apply consequences in play

[Next-session preparation](/docs/campagne/preparation) flags clocks as **almost
full** at 75% and **full** at 100%. A full clock does not write a scene or complete
a quest. Apply its consequence yourself and record what happens in the journal.

## Clock for a villain's plan (beta 1.4.0)

[The villain's plan method](/docs/campagne/methode-plan-mechant) can add a
**manual six-segment clock** to each existing playthrough. Advance it when the
enemy makes progress; it does not trigger scenes or advance automatically at
session end. A playthrough created later needs its clock added manually.
